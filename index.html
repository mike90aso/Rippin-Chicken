Good to know

Beach day questions

Where do I find you?

384 S. Atlantic Ave., Ormond Beach, FL 32176. Look for the black truck with the surfing chicken. We’re along the sand from Cardinal to Seminole and everything between.

Do you set the chairs up for us?

Yes. That’s the whole point. Tell us your spot or meet us at the truck and we’ll plant the umbrella and chairs so you can just sit down.

Can I reserve and pay online?

Yep. Pick your date, gear, and beach approach, then pay by card, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App. Prefer to pay when we set you up? That works too.

What if the weather turns?

If the beach is a bust, text us. We’ll move your reservation or take care of the refund. No weird policies, just beach sense.

What are the hours?

Typically 8 AM to 5 PM, weather and season permitting. Call or text (386) 316-3559 if you’re coming early, staying late, or booking a bigger group.
